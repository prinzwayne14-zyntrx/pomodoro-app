How statistics are calculated

Days are local calendar days on this device. This week runs Monday → today; this month is the calendar month; “last 7 days” includes today.

Focus time counts every minute actually focused, including interrupted sessions of at least 1 minute. Sessions counts completed focus sessions only. Sessions added by hand count as focus time but never earn XP.

XP: only focus sessions that run to the end and last at least 10 minutes (1 XP per minute, +10 for 20+ minutes). Streak: consecutive days with at least one XP-earning session.

Activities count on the day they happened, not the day you logged them. Totals never mix units. Editing or deleting an entry updates every statistic immediately — nothing is stored as a separate counter.
